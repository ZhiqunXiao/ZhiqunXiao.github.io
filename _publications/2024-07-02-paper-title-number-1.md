**Abstract.** Robot tasks are very often specified against an environment whose initial state the robot cannot fully observe. In such cases the robot has to sense the world and decide what to do next based on what it observes, and a plan that assumes a fully known initial state is simply not applicable.

This paper presents **OCP**, an online contingent planning method for robot tasks with incomplete knowledge. Instead of committing in advance to a complete offline plan tree, the method interleaves planning with execution: the robot updates its belief about the world from the information obtained during execution, and replans accordingly. As a result, the robot remains able to reach the goal even when its initial knowledge of the environment is incomplete.

The work was published in the proceedings of the *20th International Conference on Intelligent Computing (ICIC 2024)*, Tianjin, China, and is available as [Springer LNCS vol. 14879, pp. 39–50](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-97-5675-9_4).
